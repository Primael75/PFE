# Terms of use of external services

Conditions of the external services the project calls, checked on their own
pages. Update the date when you check again.

## Open-Meteo (weather, tool EF-14)

Checked on 2026-11-23 at https://open-meteo.com/en/terms.

- **Free API: non-commercial use only.** Open-Meteo follows the Creative
  Commons reading of "non-commercial". Public research at public
  institutions, educational content, and private or non-profit sites or apps
  without subscriptions or advertising qualify. Sites or apps with
  subscriptions or ads, and commercial products, do not.
- **Limits of the free API**: fewer than 10,000 calls per day, 5,000 per
  hour and 600 per minute (300,000 per month). Limits apply per IP address
  and per application. Open-Meteo may block abusive applications without
  notice.
- **Data licence**: CC BY 4.0, for free and paid users. Attribution is
  required; the exact wording is on Open-Meteo's licence page.
- **Commercial use** needs a paid plan (Standard, Professional or
  Enterprise).

Consequences for the project:

- The final-year project (education) fits the free API.
- A platform used by cooperatives would fit only if it stays free of
  subscriptions and advertising; otherwise it needs a paid plan. To confirm
  with Open-Meteo (info@open-meteo.com) before any deployment beyond the
  project.
- Every weather value cites Open-Meteo as its source (EF-04), which also
  meets the CC BY 4.0 attribution.
