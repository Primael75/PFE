# Cashew anthracnose

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any treatment decision.

## In short

A disease caused by a fungus, *Colletotrichum gloeosporioides*. It attacks the young tissues of the cashew tree: young leaves, shoots, flowers, apples and nuts. Warm, humid weather favours it [S1]. In West Africa, it is the only *Colletotrichum* species reported on cashew [S2].

## Symptoms

- **On leaves**: sunken lesions, roughly round or angular. They first look like oily (water-soaked) spots, then turn orange to reddish when the fungus produces its spores [S2].
- **Before flowering**, in Côte d'Ivoire: dead (necrotic) spots in a dotted pattern, or a grey area of the leaf blade that spreads over the veins [S3].
- **Early leaf fall** under heavy attack [S2].
- **On flowers**: they turn black and die [S1].
- **On young terminal shoots**: they dry out from the tip [S1].
- **On apples and nuts**: sunken lesions [S1, S2].

## Importance

- The disease hurts the harvest most when it reaches **flowers and fruits** [S1, S2].
- In Côte d'Ivoire, it was found in **every orchard studied**, with a mean national severity index of 28.48 % [S3].

## Confirmation

- Other leaf diseases occur in West African cashew orchards and can be confused with it: pestalotia leaf spot, red rust, leaf yellowing, powdery mildew, bud blight [S5].
- Only a **technician** can confirm the diagnosis in the orchard. A reliable identification of the fungus species needs a laboratory analysis [S2, S3].

## What to do

1. **Have the diagnosis confirmed** by an agricultural technician.
2. **Prune dead branches and twigs and remove them from the field**: they are the main source of infection [S1].
3. **Manage the weeds in the orchard**: in Côte d'Ivoire, *Colletotrichum* fungi taken from six orchard weeds caused anthracnose on young cashew trees, and the authors recommend taking this into account in disease control [S4].
4. **Chemical treatment**: copper-based fungicides are known to be effective against anthracnose [S1]. **No dose is given here**: the choice of product, the dose and the schedule belong to the technician and to the label of a product registered in the country.

## What the agent must not do

- Present the diagnosis as certain.
- Give a dose, a brand name or a treatment schedule.
- Say from a single photo that a tree is cured or lost.
