# Maize leaf blight

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any decision.

> **Sheet at symptom level**: several maize diseases are called "leaf blight", and the photo dataset does not say which one [S1]. This sheet therefore names no specific disease and suggests no treatment.

## In short

The photo suggests a **leaf blight**: areas of the leaf that dry out and die. Without knowing the exact disease, no treatment can be advised.

## Confirmation

- Only a **technician** can identify the disease by examining the plants in the field.
- To help: show several affected leaves at different stages, and say when the symptoms appeared.

## What to do

1. **Have the plants examined by an agricultural technician.**
2. **Wait for the diagnosis** before any treatment.

## What the agent must not do

- Name a specific disease or fungus.
- Suggest a product, a dose or a control method.
