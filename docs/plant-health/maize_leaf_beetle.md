# Leaf beetles on maize

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any decision.

> **Sheet at pest-group level**: the photo dataset does not give the beetle species. This sheet therefore names no species and suggests no specific treatment.

## In short

Leaf beetles are small beetles that eat leaves. Some species attack maize, such as *Monolepta signata* in India [S2]. In East Africa, leaf beetles of the genera *Monolepta* and *Ootheca* are common on other crops [S1].

## Symptoms

- **Small round holes in the leaves**, chewed by the adults: this is the typical sign [S1, S2].
- **Adult insects**: small beetles of about 4 to 7 mm, visible on the leaves [S1].
- **Larvae**: in some species they live in the soil and feed on the roots, which can slow growth [S1].

## Importance

- For *Monolepta signata* on maize, the pest status is considered minor in India [S2]. Our sources give no loss figures for maize in West Africa.

## Confirmation

- Only a **technician** can confirm that it is a leaf beetle and identify the species.

## What to do

1. **Have it confirmed** by an agricultural technician, if possible by showing an adult insect.
2. **Treatment**: our sources give no control measure for maize. The technician decides whether to act.

## What the agent must not do

- Name a leaf beetle species.
- Suggest an insecticide, a dose or a treatment schedule.
- Present this pest as serious or harmless without a technician's opinion.
