# Cassava green mite

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any decision.

## In short

A pest: a very small mite, *Mononychellus tanajoa*, that feeds on cassava leaves, mostly on their underside [S1]. It reached Africa in 1971 and had spread across the cassava belt by 1985 [S1]. Its populations peak in the dry season, because rain washes the mites off the leaves [S1].

## Symptoms

- **On leaves**: yellowing, mottling, then death and fall of the leaves [S1].
- **On shoots**: loss of the shoot tips, which gives the stem a "candle stick" look [S1].
- **To check**: look for yellowing and leaf loss on the shoot tips, then look along the veins under the leaves with a hand lens [S1].

## Importance

- Depending on the variety and the length of the dry season, losses can reach 50 % of leaf weight and 80 % of root yield [S1].
- Weak plants with thin stems also give poorer cuttings for the next crop [S1].

## Confirmation

- Cassava mosaic gives yellow and green patches that do not follow the veins and deforms the leaves, but causes no "candle stick" [S1].
- Cassava bacterial blight gives angular black spots on the leaves [S1].
- Only a **technician** can confirm the diagnosis.

## What to do

1. **Have it confirmed** by an agricultural technician.
2. **Protect natural enemies**: the predatory mite *Typhlodromalus aripo*, introduced in 1993, is the main predator of the green mite in the African cassava belt [S1]. In Benin, a field study confirmed that it controls the green mite well [S3]. A fungus, *Neozygites tanajoae*, also attacks the green mite and is found in Benin cassava fields alongside this predator [S2].
3. **Take cuttings from healthy plants** [S1].
4. **Plant early in the rainy season** [S1].
5. **Intercrop with pigeon pea** [S1].
6. **Tolerant varieties** have been bred by IITA (Ibadan, Nigeria): ask the technician which ones are available [S1].
7. **Chemical treatment**: not recommended by our source. It is too costly, it can make mites resistant, and it kills the natural enemies [S1].

## What the agent must not do

- Present the diagnosis as certain.
- Suggest a product, a dose or a treatment schedule.
