# Tomato leaf blight

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any treatment decision.

> **Sheet at symptom level**: several tomato diseases are called "blight" [S1], and the photo dataset does not say which one. This sheet therefore names no specific disease and suggests no specific treatment.

## In short

The photo suggests a **leaf blight** on tomato. In Ghana, a survey found several fungal leaf diseases of tomato in major production areas [S2].

## Confirmation

- Only a **technician** can identify the disease. Some tomato blights spread very fast in favourable weather [S1]: call one without delay.

## What to do

1. **Have the plants examined by an agricultural technician**, quickly.
2. **General measures against tomato leaf diseases**, useful whatever the disease [S1]:
   - use healthy transplants;
   - practise a long rotation without tomato;
   - remove crop debris and do not compost it;
   - stake and prune the plants so that the foliage dries;
   - water at the foot of the plant, not on the leaves.
3. **Treatment**: no product or dose is given here. The technician decides after the diagnosis.

## What the agent must not do

- Name a specific disease or fungus.
- Suggest a fungicide, a dose or a treatment schedule.
