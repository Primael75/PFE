# Cashew red rust

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any treatment decision.

## In short

A disease caused by a parasitic green alga of the genus *Cephaleuros*, not by a fungus [S1]. It was identified on cashew in Ghana, in the Bono region [S1], and in Togo and Mali (*C. virescens*) [S2, S3]. Rain favours it [S1].

## Symptoms

- **On the upper side of leaves**: round spots, raised or flat, velvety, orange-brown, rust-coloured or grey-green [S1].
- **Size**: from mere dots to spots of about 3 mm, with threadlike edges [S1].
- **Underside**: no symptom observed in Ghana [S1].
- **Heavy attack**: deformed leaves, leaf fall, dieback of twigs [S1].

## Importance

- In Ghana (Dormaa Central, Bono region), the disease was present in every orchard visited, with an incidence of 2 % to 26 % depending on the orchard and a severity of 0.2 to 5 on a 0 to 9 scale [S1].
- It appears in the rainy season: no attack was recorded in the dry season [S1].

## Confirmation

- In Ghana, identification combined the observation of symptoms with a microscope examination of the alga [S1].
- Only a **technician** can confirm the diagnosis.

## What to do

1. **Have the diagnosis confirmed** by an agricultural technician.
2. **Open up the orchard**: keep enough space between trees and avoid excessive shade, because humidity and poor air flow favour the disease [S1].
3. **Clean the orchard**: remove plant debris, weed, remove climbing plants and prune affected branches [S1].
4. **Strengthen the trees**: good nutrition and good soil drainage [S1].
5. **Avoid wounding the trees** during field work [S1].
6. **Chemical treatment**: copper-based products are cited by other studies reviewed in the Ghanaian source [S1]. **No dose is given here**: the technician decides whether to treat.

## What the agent must not do

- Present the diagnosis as certain.
- Give a dose, a concentration or a number of applications.
- Confuse this disease, caused by an alga, with a rust caused by a fungus.
