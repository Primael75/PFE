# Cassava brown leaf spot

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any decision.

## In short

A leaf disease caused by a fungus, *Passalora henningsii*. It mostly affects the older, lower leaves and becomes more visible five to six months after planting [S1]. Warm, humid weather makes it worse [S1].

## Symptoms

- **Spots**: round at first, up to 15 mm across, then angular because the veins limit them [S1].
- **Upper side of the leaf**: brown spots with dark borders, sometimes with a faint yellow margin [S1].
- **Underside**: grey spots with less sharp borders [S1].
- **Small veins** crossing a spot look like black lines [S1].
- **Centre of the spots**: it dries, cracks and may fall out [S1].
- **Heavy attack**: leaves turn yellow and fall early [S1].

## How it spreads

- Spores form on the underside of leaves and spread by wind and rain splash [S1].
- Over long distances, through planting material [S1].
- The fungus survives on old fallen leaves [S1].

## Importance

- Root yield losses of up to 30 % have been reported in Africa [S1].
- Damage usually comes late in the crop and stays on the older leaves [S1].

## Confirmation

- Only a **technician** can confirm the diagnosis.

## What to do

1. **Have the diagnosis confirmed** by an agricultural technician.
2. **Plant with wide spacing**, so that wind dries the leaves quickly [S1].
3. **Rotate crops** for three to five years [S1].
4. **Do not plant a new crop next to an old diseased one** [S1].
5. **In the dry season, remove heavily diseased leaves** [S1].
6. **After harvest, collect and burn crop debris** [S1].
7. **Varieties**: compare varieties and keep those that lose the fewest leaves [S1].
8. **Chemical treatment**: the source considers the disease minor; if control is really needed, it names copper fungicides or mancozeb [S1]. **No dose is given here**: the technician decides, following the label of a product registered in the country.

## What the agent must not do

- Present the diagnosis as certain.
- Give a dose, a brand name or a treatment schedule.
