# Tomato leaf curl

> This is a **suspicion** based on a photo. It must be **confirmed by an agricultural technician** before any decision.

> **Sheet at symptom level**: the photo dataset groups these images under the name "leaf curl" without giving the cause [S1]. This sheet therefore names no cause and suggests no treatment.

## In short

The photo suggests **curled tomato leaves**. This symptom can have several causes, and only a field examination can tell which one.

## Confirmation

- Only a **technician** can identify the cause.
- To help: show young and old leaves, and mention any small insects seen on the plants.

## What to do

1. **Have the plants examined by an agricultural technician.**
2. **Wait for the diagnosis** before any treatment.

## What the agent must not do

- Name a specific disease, virus or insect.
- Suggest a product, a dose or a control method.
