# markets

## Purpose
Market prices, net gain of each selling option (sell here now, store,
sell on another market), price forecast at one to three months, and a
price checker for a buyer's offer.

## Owner
Team 3 (Markets).

## Provides
Price trend per crop, to team 1.

- Price tools: sprint 2.
